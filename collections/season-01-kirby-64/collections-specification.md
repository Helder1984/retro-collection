## Objective

The second collection of the Retro Collection project.

Created to celebrate Kirby 64: The Crystal Shards and expand the Retro Collection catalog through a complete set of collectible cards featuring the game's characters, enemies, bosses, abilities, and memorable moments.

The collection follows the visual and production standards established in Season 00.
